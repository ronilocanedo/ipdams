## Owner requirement and feature scope

The owner requests **Stage A: Itinerary of Travel (Appendix 45) → documentary travel reimbursement claim**, and additionally requests that **expenses without official receipts** be identified **per individual expense component** and shown in **a different report**.

- In **Travel → Reimbursement → Prepare reimbursement claim**, enter **1–60 dated itinerary legs** with place/route, optional times and transportation mode, and amounts for **fare, per diem and other expenses**.
- Each **nonzero** component is separately labeled **Receipt available**, **No official receipt**, or **Alternative support (no official receipt)**. Store optional evidence reference and no-receipt explanation, not just one receipt flag per itinerary row. This prevents a fare's OR from incorrectly covering a same-row per diem.
- Server-side PHP derives claimed category subtotals and **claimed total** from the detailed itinerary; it does not trust client totals or classify claimed money as automatically allowable. The structured itinerary and source paper reference are stored in an **RC document version** already linked to the actual **AR → TA**, preserving immutable versions/audit. Existing legacy claims remain in their former editor format.
- A signed actual Appendix 45 scan may be uploaded privately to the RC draft as the optional `actual_itinerary` evidence kind; it is mandatory before executing the **existing** documentary review on new-format claims, not before saving the draft.
- A saved itinerary is displayed read-only in the reopened claim, with receipt/evidence status beneath each component.
- **Expenses Without Receipts · Separate Report** is a scoped read-only report listing only `NO_RECEIPT` and `ALTERNATIVE` monetary components (with both categories explicitly distinguished), including employee, TA, paper itinerary reference, date, route, expense category, amount, evidence reference and notes. Separate **print** (A4 landscape external stylesheet), **CSV** with formula-injection escaping, and **40-claim pagination** with load-older are available. The issued-only Division MOOE VIEWER role is explicitly rejected for private travel details.
- **No receipts does not mean automatically reimbursable**, and alternative evidence does not automatically prove an entitlement. The entire module remains **NON-POSTING**: does not create or finalize a DV, authorize or settle payment, post a journal, allocate a fund, or alter original signed paper evidence.
