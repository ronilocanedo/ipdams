## Behavior

Within Travel & Training → Reimbursement → Expense Review → Review Expense floating editor, **Paper Reviewer Name/Position, Evidence / Paper Reference and Review Explanation are optional** for normal allowed, partially allowed and excluded decisions. A coded Reason remains required, and amount/status reconciliation remains strict. Existing review versions, claims and financial flows are not rewritten.

An exception remains for an allowance granted to a fare overlapping a `DOCUMENTED_ACTUAL` seminar session: the reason must be `AUTHORIZED_MOVEMENT`, with supporting evidence reference either already on the underlying Actual Itinerary expense or entered in the expense review. This protects documented authorized-movement findings without duplicate entry for ordinary expenses.

The reviewing account identity is still recorded by the backend in `review_recorded_by`, review-version `actor_id`, timestamps and audited write events even when manually entered reviewer name is blank. DTE remains receipt exempt and out of CENRR. No claims, accounting entries, voucher, payment or fund posting changes.
