## Owner finding

In installed v0.2.274, users can save a draft but cannot dispose of an incorrect, unwanted or redundant unverified book draft. Saving correctly retains a draft until it is verified, but there was no discard endpoint or control.

## Change

- **Discard draft** action, with a second confirmation step and **Keep draft** cancel option, on editable draft rows and inside the editor.
- Only the registering school and its supervising Division (or the registering Division itself) can discard its unverified draft. The backend enforces scope and version checks under a shared catalog write lock.
- Draft is **soft discarded**: latest TBP snapshot becomes `DISCARDED`, with authenticated actor, office and timestamp, plus append-only document/audit records. No hard delete or physical stock mutation.
- Discarded profiles disappear from active directory and pending-identity search. The identifier becomes available for a new authentic registration, but a discarded profile cannot be republished or edited.
- **Show discarded drafts** is available to the originating school and its Division only; archived record read-only details and version history remain accessible. Other schools cannot see these archived details.
- Verified books cannot be discarded, prior TBR receipts remain intact, and registration still never adds stock.
- Add previous-page navigation to Book Directory profile list.
