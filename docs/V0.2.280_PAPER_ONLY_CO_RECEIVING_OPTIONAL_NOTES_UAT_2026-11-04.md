## Why the update was requested

The owner reported an error while receiving books in v0.2.279 and no clear field for the requested note. Source review confirmed `TextbookCoReceiptWorkflow::inspect` required `inspection_note` of 15–500 characters while the frontend did not clearly indicate that lower bound. The owner further clarified that IPDAMS **documents actual physical inspection and receiving already evidenced on paper**, and must not require each named inspector or Property Custodian to log in, approve, or electronically sign.

## Working behavior in this release

- A scoped authenticated office user creates a source-linked CO receipt draft, then opens **Review → Record Physical Receipt & Update Stock**. No separate electronic submit/inspect/accept workflow or property-custodian login is required.
- Form asks for **actual inspecting officer**, **actual receiving person**, their optional positions, actual inspection/receipt dates, optional paper references, reconciled delivered/serviceable/damaged/held counts, and a data-entry transcription confirmation (not an e-signature).
- **Inspection observations / notes** is an explicitly optional, visible multiline field (0–500 characters) and appears on the linked printed IAR if supplied. New draft receipt remarks are optional (0–500 characters).
- The actual inspector and receiver are displayed separately on the IAR; the logged-in encoder is stored separately in the audited version. An actual person may be named in both printed roles if consistent with genuine documentary records.
- Preserve transactional once-only stock posting only after recorded physical receipt; separate serviceable and quarantined lots; held and missing never enter the ledger. Reject duplicate attempts.
- DRAFT/SUBMITTED/INSPECTED legacy receipts may complete directly. Corrections to previously inspected counts require recorded reason. A fully rejected physical delivery can be marked rejected directly from DRAFT with genuine paper reason and reference, without stock posting.
- Existing `IAR` serial allocation, TBR immutable history, original procurement isolation, global ISBN picker, and Book Directory remain unchanged.
