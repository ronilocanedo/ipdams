## Key design decision
A **Travel Reimbursement** DV must be a distinct source. Existing `REIMBURSEMENT` DVs are reimbursements of procurement advances and must not be reused.

- One employee, one organization and one fund per DV; multiple eligible unpaid, reviewed claims allowed.
- Preserve separately: original claimed amount, preliminary amount allowed, independent accounting approval, prior paid/advanced amounts, and net eligible for DV.
- Reviewed documentary claim is not yet accounting approval: `ready_for_dv=false` and `payment_authorized=false` in actual v0.2.325 review code.
- Prevent duplicate DV reservations via atomic locks, a database-enforced unique active-claim source key, immutable claim/review/approval versions, and safe cancellation/reversal/paid-state reconciliation.
- Finalization must integrate exact current Domain DV source validation, manual accounting/UACS, fund controls, Appendix 32, check payments and audit events; no financial state is modified by the source-only planner.
