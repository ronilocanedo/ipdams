## Specification

The printed Appendix 47 "Explanation or justifications" derives its standard description from (1) the actual purpose of travel in the linked Activity Request (fallback to title), (2) the **seminar/activity start and end dates** in the saved Temporary Itinerary plan, and (3) the actual venue and address/destination in the linked source records and planned sessions. **Never use the TA/Actual Itinerary start/end travel period as the activity period.**

The edit window shows the derived context, with a warning if activity dates are missing. For "Strictly in accordance" an empty explanation is prefilled and remains editable. Existing nonempty explanations remain unchanged. When cut short, extended, or with deviations, a separate actual explanation remains mandatory; the generated activity text must not satisfy that reason. The printout of a previously saved blank explanation can display derived official-source context; reopening and saving records it as a versioned certificate.

No modification to printed Appendix 47 styling, evidence, signatures, document-compliance requirements, finance, DTE, DV, or payment. No schema migrations.
