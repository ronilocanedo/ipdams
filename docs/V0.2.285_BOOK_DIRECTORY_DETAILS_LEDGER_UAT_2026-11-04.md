## Function

A new **Details** button beside the Book Directory’s Edit/View button opens a separate read-only ledger in the floating dialog UI. The edition is anchored by the verified book profile’s immutable `title_id`, **not** by a free-text title match. Displays ISBN/grade/edition; lifetime quantities received/opening, transferred in/out, distributed/issued, returned, disposed, on-hand by condition and batch, and teacher outstanding. Separates pending loss, reported teacher damage, disposal proposals and quarantine documentation from actual stock postings. Shows dated movement evidence and source references in pages of 100 while lifetime totals remain unaffected by pagination. Unlinked drafts or LR-code-only verified profiles show a non-posting explanation.

The default ledger is **the authenticated selected office**. A Division user may explicitly choose **Division and supervised schools** for a consolidated historical view of active supervised schools and its own division. Schools cannot request that view. Source profiles retain original visibility restrictions, including private drafts. All queries are read-only and every scope is checked server-side.
