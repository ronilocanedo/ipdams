## Changes

Temporary Itinerary → Add Expenses: a wider compact modal, always-visible Transport/DTE/Others amounts, and immediate item-specific proof status, time incurred, purpose and reference underneath any positive amount. DTE shows **receipt exempt** with supporting fields; no general receipt classification is offered for DTE.

Route input now has editable From and To. On a new entry, the preceding row's explicit To location and recorded Time To prefill the next From and Time From. No arrival or destination is invented. The existing combined `places` field persists `From → To`; editing an old record's amounts without changing the route preserves its original text. Date remains editable. No database changes or financial posting.
