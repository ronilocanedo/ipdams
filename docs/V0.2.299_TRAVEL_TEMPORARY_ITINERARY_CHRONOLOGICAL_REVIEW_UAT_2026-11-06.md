## Behavior

- **Temporary itinerary:** pre-trip versioned estimates linked to a real AR/TA and scoped employee/organization; estimated fares, daily travel expenses, other; separate expected receipt/no-receipt/alternative/unknown proof per nonzero component; program schedule; chronological planned expense register and TEMPORARY-only print. Never populates/approves the Division's official handwritten/countersigned AR amount.
- **Actual Appendix 45:** optional copying of an estimate into an editable actual form only after trip, with every expected receipt/proof/time cleared. New actual per-component expense time and official purpose are optional; old claims remain readable.
- **Chronological actual expense review:** all with-/without-receipt components sorted by genuine date/time, seminar sessions and location; status flags for in-session fare; independently entered preliminary ALLOWED, PARTIALLY_ALLOWED, EXCLUDED, NEEDS_EVIDENCE or UNREVIEWED findings, preserving claimed amounts and auditable prior snapshots.
- A documented actual seminar-overlapping fare cannot be tentatively allowed unless it has an explicit paper-referenced AUTHORIZED_MOVEMENT finding; apparent time overlap is a review flag, not automatic deletion or financial judgment.
- No DV, bank/check, UACS, fund, obligation, payment, payable amount, supplier or procurement-advance reimbursement changes.
