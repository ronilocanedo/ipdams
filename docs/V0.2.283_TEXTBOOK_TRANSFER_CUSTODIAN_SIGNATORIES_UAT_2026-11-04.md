## Owner-directed behavior

Textbook transfers, whether **Option A** (allocation / school request / issuing office dispatch) or **Option B** (no-notice direct dispatch), are physical transactions documented inside IPDAMS. No electronic sign-in, signature or approval by the persons named on the paper RIS or ITR.

- For Division-issued transfers, **issuing official defaults to the date-effective Division Property Custodian** appointed in Reference Data → Employees. For school-to-school transfers, issuer is **the issuing school's own Property Custodian**.
- Receiving-school Property Custodian is identified at dispatch and appears on linked RIS and ITR **before** the school records physical receipt. At receiving time the school receiver is prefilled from its **date-effective** Property Custodian appointment. The Option A requester likewise defaults to the school's custodian; it can be edited to match the physical request.
- The encoder may enter a different **acting** issuer or receiver, but must record a genuine authority/reference or reason of **at least 8 characters**. The appointed custodian is preserved alongside the actual acting name in the versioned TBX snapshot. The RIS/ITR contain separate manual countersignature lines, without implying an electronic attestation.
- Appointments resolve through existing `IarSignatories::resolve`; no duplicate personnel registry or guessing of unappointed officers. If absent, prompt configuration and allow accurately recording the actual physical paper name rather than falsely claiming an appointment.
- Existing transfer versions and documents are not overwritten. No changes to stock reservations, dispatch debit, receiving credit, quantity limits, serial numbering, office access, CO receipts, Property Accountability, procurement, or finance.
