## Requirement and behavior

The owner clarified that **“Posted by”** on the **Report of Semi-Expendable Property Issued** must identify the **Division MOOE in-charge assigned to the school**, not the person who clicked Print nor the school's Property Custodian. The left **“I hereby certify”** section remains the school Property Custodian.

The app already has the **Reference Data → Division MOOE Assignments** effective-dated appointment registry and authenticated, school-scoped `division.mooe.assignments` GET endpoint. Rather than inventing a new signer field, v0.2.295 reuses it:

- Existing GET endpoint accepts **optional `as_of` YYYY-MM-DD** after validating it as a real date no later than today. Omission retains its preexisting “today” behavior. The existing `$this->org($p)` scope/role checks remain.
- On semi-expendable report preparation, query the appointment **effective on the source ICS issuance date**, choose only a school row whose ID equals the active school workspace, and render the stored Division employee's name and designation. No fallback to a different school's appointment or today’s assignee for older reports.
- If no dated assignment exists, or the read fails, leave Posted by name/designation blank and provide a preview-only configuration warning. Do not guess or silently use the school custodian.
- Add a distinct **blank signature line** and blank posting date. Printed identity is **not** evidence of a signed document, posting, or accounting entry.
- Continue printing UACS account title as small italic text below verified UACS codes, preserve formal Appendix 64 tables, certification panel, and all Print ICS/RIS/RIS+ICS actions.
