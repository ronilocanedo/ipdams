## Purpose and semantics
Division Consolidation previously selected only user-scoped schools. The actual Division Office was absent. An accepted interoffice transfer creates a new receiving-office asset with `organization_id` of the destination and `status='ACTIVE'`; its sender asset remains preserved with `status='TRANSFERRED'`. The current Asset Register previously included both without clarifying historical ownership.

Version 0.2.267:
1. Adds the active Division Office to the consolidation's read-only office scope, alongside personally scoped active schools.
2. For DIVISION users, all own-office categories are accessible through existing Division scope; school category grants and school membership remain unchanged. SUPER_ADMIN still sees all Division schools inside the chosen Division.
3. Supports selecting Division Office or a school through the existing `school_id` parameter, validated against the authorized office list (no arbitrary organization-ID bypass).
4. Asset Register and its overview count include **only ACTIVE assets** for the current holding office, excluding transferred-out historical sender rows; no duplicate asset totals.
5. Adds **registration origin** (Transferred in / Accepted IAR / Historical opening), **condition**, and clearer **holding office** column.
6. ICS/PAR and asset movement views are dated **historical documents**; they are not reassigned or deleted when property transfers. Source transfer provenance remains in existing Property History.
7. Division supply, RIS, receipts, textbooks and current stock totals are now included for the Division Office as well. Counts named `school_count` in the internal legacy response are displayed as **Offices** because they now cover Division + schools.
8. No property edits, accounting posts, migration, permissions writes, cross-Division data access, or asset remapping.
