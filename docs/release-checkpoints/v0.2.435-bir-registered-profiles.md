# IPDAMS v0.2.435 — BIR registered identity profiles

**Development checkpoint (2026-11-25).** Forward Settings update from v0.2.434. No migrations. **Not deployed by this GitHub commit.**

## Feature

- **Organization → Division Profile:** separate registered BIR name, TIN, address and ZIP. The operational Division name is unchanged. For example, the BIR name can be `Department of Education` while the IPDAMS name remains `Division of City Schools – Tagbilaran City`.
- **Organization → School Profile:** IUS schools maintain their own registered BIR identity. School users can manage the identity for their authorized IUS school.
- **Non-IUS:** the school inherits the parent Division's BIR name, TIN, address and ZIP. The school-level representation is read-only, preventing shared-TIN overrides.
- **Signatory:** both types retain the assigned **School Head** and the Head's personal TIN from the linked employee profile. The registered payor TIN is never confused with that personal TIN.
- **Storage:** reuses the existing `BIR2307_PAYOR_IDENTITY_V1` configuration versions, optimistic locking, authorization checks and audit events. No separate TIN store or schema migration.
- **Readiness:** requires confirmation of the BIR registered name rather than silently printing the operational Division name. Tax figures come from saved DVs; no recalculation or posting.
