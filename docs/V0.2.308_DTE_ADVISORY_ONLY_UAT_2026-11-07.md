## Design
Allow staff to record the full claimed DTE even if it exceeds the applicable regional/day reference. No mandatory explanation, new policy authority, or effective date is needed merely to save the documentary claim. In Add Expenses and the saved expense row, show the amount above the reference. Server saves claimed amount, applicable reference, excess and independent-review flags. No automatic entitlement or payment approval. Unknown distance, additional transit days and NIR are marked for manual review rather than misleadingly labeled as over a zero-peso ceiling.

Preserve existing financial/payment safeguards, original estimates, duplicate-DTE-date restrictions, per-diem receipt exemption, CENRR exclusion and prior itinerary revisions.
