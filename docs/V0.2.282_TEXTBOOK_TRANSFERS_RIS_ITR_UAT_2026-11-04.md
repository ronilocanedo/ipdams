## Owner-approved workflows

**Option A: Allocation → Receiving-school request → Issuance → Receipt.**
Division Office or issuing School allocates *serviceable, unreserved* copies to a designated school in the same Division. Allocation holds available quantity but does not debit stock. Receiving school sees an Action Center allocation, reviews each book, selects requested quantity, **preferred delivery mode and preferred delivery date**, and sends its documentary request. Issuing office sees incoming request, records actual issue quantities/date/mode and paper releasing officer; at dispatch it generates distinct printable **RIS and ITR** serials and debits only issued source stock. Receiving school acknowledges actual physically received serviceable/damaged counts using real paper recipient identity. Only received counts credit destination stock; partial/in-transit remains traceable.

**Option B: Direct transfer without advance notice.**
Issuer selects destination school and serviceable stock and **records actual issuer delivery** directly, with no advance school allocation or request. Source stock debited at documented dispatch with linked printable RIS and ITR; the recipient sees an Action Center incoming transfer **only after the dispatch record commits**, and subsequently records actual paper receipt to credit school stock. No electronic approvals, signatory logins or false procurement PO.

## Paper documents and safeguards

- Both source Division and source School supported, destination an active School within the same parent Division.
- Per-source lot reservations, source organization mutex/serialized writes, read-committed SQL transaction, original global API idempotency keys, destination-bound receipt, strict counts and audit-snapshotted document versions.
- Separate serviceable/damaged-quarantined book lots on receipt, with in-transit balance documented until properly acknowledged. No double credit; no artificial school balances.
- RIS/ITR allocated from existing annual school numbering; generated paper layouts have blank physical-signature sections and **do not claim digital signatures or electronic approvals**.
- The received paper names and preference vs actual dispatch fields remain visible in audit and print, separate from logged-in encoders.
- Action Center queries **pending** snapshots independently (bounded 500) so 50 newer historical rows cannot hide an older outstanding transfer. Main register currently displays latest 50, with older paging deferred.
- Distinct new `TBX` document type, independent of procurement and legacy generic book movement tools. Avoid false PO, DV or inventory credits.
