## Functional behavior
- The receiving office's **Received Asset Register** shows **Assess condition** when the logged-in user is the currently appointed/bound Property Custodian; a Super Administrator may configure the binding, but cannot act as the custodian.
- A property-only form requires inspection date, actual condition, evidence reference, 12+ characters of findings/reason, and an affirmative physical-inspection attestation.
- The server independently verifies active organization, exact role and bound/appointed user, explicit scope, verified two-office transfer/receipt provenance, ACTIVE and unassigned receiving property, no open ICS/PAR, and no conflicting formal lifecycle/unserviceable/disposal status.
- Save locks the organization and asset; checks current binding/version, expected old condition and inspection date, then modifies **only** receiving `assets.condition_note` and inserts a before/after `audit_events` record in one transaction. A failed audit rolls back the change.
- Existing original ITR/PTR transfer evidence, condition on receipt, source property, original IAR, permanent tag, custody history, issued ICS/PAR snapshots, numbering, accounting, stock, funds, journal and checks are unchanged.
- An ICS/PAR remains a **separate authorized action**, and the existing `Serviceable` requirement for received property remains in place.
