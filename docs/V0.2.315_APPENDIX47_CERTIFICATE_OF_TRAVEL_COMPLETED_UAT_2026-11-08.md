## Basis
DepEd Division Memorandum No. 202 s. 2023 (SDO Lanao del Norte) includes Appendix 47 — Certification of Travel Completed, associated with Appendix 45. The Appendix 47 fields and signature blocks were adapted to the current IPDAMS source and school/Division signatory configuration.

## Change
Reimbursement → Documents adds a compact floating **Appendix 47 Certificate of Travel Completed** editor. The claimant, Travel Authority reference and actual dates are drawn from the saved RC/TA records. One of four conditions is selected (as authorized, cut short, extended, other deviation); deviations require justification. Paper evidence references are text-only with no file upload. Refund claims require the actual OR number and date when positive; the system never creates cash refund or payment records.

Saving creates an audited, versioned **unsigned draft** in the existing RC document snapshot (no migration). The SDS name from the TA snapshot appears under an unsigned approval line; the school head or ASDS appears as station-in-charge when grounded. Missing signatories are flagged. Printing follows an A4 portrait Appendix 47 layout using Bookman Old Style fallback. Updating claim data invalidates the current certificate (historical version remains). Saving a certificate invalidates the prior paper-compliance checklist for explicit re-confirmation. After Division documentary verification the certificate is read-only.

**Safety:** no DV, obligations, fund events, bank transactions, automatic documentary checks, approval or electronic signatures. All production financial controls remain unchanged.
