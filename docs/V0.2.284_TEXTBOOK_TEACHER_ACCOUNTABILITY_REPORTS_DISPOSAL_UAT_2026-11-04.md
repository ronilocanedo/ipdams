## User-requested scope

- **Textbooks → Teacher Issuance**: issue actual school stock to a real teacher named from staff directory or handwritten paper, record physical date/reference, printable teacher accountability slip with manual signature lines. Serviceable stock debits once on issue; quantity remains accountable to teacher. Serviceable return replenishes stock, damaged return enters quarantine, partial returns preserve balance. A reported loss remains accountable pending separate relief procedure, with **no second stock debit**. Damage while still with teacher is documentary until physical return.
- **Textbooks → Disposal**: document physical damage and quarantine, mark specific school/Division stock batch for disposal (stock hold only), and send proposals to supervising Division **Action Center**. Division staff records genuine written authority or denial (reference/date/named official), **not an electronic-signature approval**. Only after recorded written Division authority can the owning office choose **Record Disposed**, capturing actual method, date, quantity, paper evidence and optional remarks. Actual disposal debits on-hand stock once, permits partial disposal, protects allocations and teacher issues against held stock. An incorrect pending proposal can be **Withdrawn** with an audited reason before Division acts.
- **Textbooks → Reports**: available/held/quarantined stock, teacher accountability, teacher-reported lost/damaged books, damaged-stock quarantine register, disposal register (including last disposal date/method/reference), inter-office textbook transfers and movement history. Client-side print and safe CSV export.
- Paper-based system: named physical custodians, teachers and officials do not log in or approve electronically. User/scoped encoder audit stored on documents. The app **does not** replace any required external Division, committee, COA or other lawful written authorizations. No fake procurement IAR/PO, financial records, or changes to existing CO receiving or RIS/ITR.
