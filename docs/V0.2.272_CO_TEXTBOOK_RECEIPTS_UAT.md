## Scope

This first working patch implements the physical intake paths approved by the owner:

1. **CO → Division:** The Division records the actual Central Office shipment. Only the Division Office's textbook balance increases upon appointed receiving custodian acceptance. Division distribution to schools will be a *later* separate transaction with each school's acceptance.
2. **CO → School direct:** The school records the Central Office shipment in its own workspace. Only that school stock increases on acceptance. The Division receives no mirror receipt and no phantom Division stock.
3. **Shared ISBN directory registration:** An authorized Division or school user may add a valid ISBN edition with exact title, grade, subject and authentic bibliographic/CO reference, explicitly confirming the source. Registration is audited, serialized globally, and rejects existing canonical ISBN-10/ISBN-13 equivalents. It **never credits stock**.
4. **Documentary workflow:** Draft → Submitted → Inspected → Accepted / Accepted with Discrepancy. Pre-acceptance drafts and inspections have no stock movement; a signed rejection or draft cancellation also posts nothing. All states are append-only snapshots.
5. **Stock condition:** Accepted serviceable copies are separately entered into serviceable lots; physically accepted damaged copies enter *Damaged - Quarantined* lots. Held/missing copies are not credited. The stock register displays the lot condition. Existing unsafe generic textbook opening/transfer/loss/adjustment writes remain blocked.

## Architecture, security, and remaining boundaries

- This patch deliberately **avoids new database tables**. `TBR` is an **internal IPDAMS textbook receipt reference**, allocated using existing year-continuous `document_counters` / `document_registry`. Every state appends to existing `document_versions`. Final acceptance locks the receipt and atomically posts condition-specific `textbook_batches` and `textbook_movements`; replay protection uses the API's existing route+payload idempotency key. Signed paper references and audit events are preserved.
- An authenticated receiving school/Division role, current office scope, valid original ISBN, signed inspection reference, different inspector, and explicitly linked Property Custodian are checked server-side. Technical `SUPER_ADMIN` cannot act as a receiving custodian.
- Duplicate CO delivery references are serialized per office; the same external CO reference can legitimately be used by two separate receiving offices with independent real deliveries. ISBN canonical registration is serialized across all organizations under one stable database row lock.
- The installer did **not** change settings migration policy or the reset/backup table whitelist, because the patch needs no new SQL tables. There is no ad hoc SQL or separate staging website.
- This patch **does not yet** implement Division-to-school dispatch/acceptance, surplus offers, teacher accountability, learner-level SF3, damaged/lost/disposal approvals, upload of attachments, or a formally approved PDF/print CO receipt form. Those workflows must be separate, audited subsequent changes. Do not use the old generic posting controls to simulate them.
- The official delivery paperwork, who may sign inspection, and any specific approval authority must still follow the applicable Central Office/Division instructions. IPDAMS records document references; it does not certify a wet signature automatically.
